# UKCS Field Economics and Operational Transparency Analytics

## Level

Scope - Reproducible data analytics, business intelligence and economic screening.

## Business question

How did field production, the Brent benchmark price, gas flaring and produced-water burden shape the relative operational and economic performance of UKCS oil fields during 2021-2025?

## Required outcomes

1. Build a reproducible clean field-month dataset.
2. Join monthly UKCS production to the monthly Brent benchmark.
3. Calculate transparent production, revenue-proxy and operating-efficiency indicators.
4. Compare fields and operators without presenting gross revenue as profit.
5. Communicate the results through tables, charts and an interactive dashboard.

## Acceptance criteria

- The pipeline runs from the supplied raw and reference data.
- Every project month has a Brent benchmark price.
- Zero-production rows do not create infinite intensity ratios.
- Five charts and five analysis tables are generated.
- Automated tests pass.
- The documentation identifies all major assumptions and limitations.
