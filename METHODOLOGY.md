# Methodology and limitations

## Core calculations

- Oil barrels = oil production in cubic metres x 6.28981077.
- Gross oil revenue proxy = monthly oil barrels x monthly Brent spot price.
- Flaring intensity = 1,000 x gas flared in thousand standard cubic metres / oil produced in cubic metres.
- Water-oil ratio = produced-water volume / oil volume.
- Annual change = percentage change in annual oil barrels between consecutive years.

## Interpretation limits

The gross revenue proxy is not field profit, cash flow, net present value or valuation. It excludes realised crude differentials, gas and condensate revenue, hedging, transport, operating expenditure, capital expenditure, decommissioning expenditure, tax, royalties, downtime attribution and ownership shares.

Flaring volume is not a carbon-dioxide-equivalent emissions measure. It is used only as a transparent operational indicator. Water-oil ratio is also an operational burden indicator, not a direct cost.

Brent is a common benchmark and may differ from the realised price of a specific crude stream. Values are shown in nominal US dollars without inflation or currency conversion.

The reported operator can change over time. Field summaries retain the latest operator label in the selected period and should not be interpreted as historical ownership attribution.

Intensity ratios are left undefined where oil production is zero. Charts may exclude the highest five per cent of flaring-intensity observations to keep the portfolio pattern readable; the underlying tables remain uncensored.
